FAQ

Good to know before you order

Where do you deliver, and how fast?

We currently deliver across Bangkok, with same-day delivery on orders placed before 2 PM. Outside Bangkok, delivery may take 1–2 extra days depending on the courier partner.

What payment methods do you accept?

PromptPay/QR, major credit and debit cards, and popular e-wallets. You can pay through the website or LINE checkout.

How fresh are the products, and how should I store them?

Everything is made to order in small batches and delivered chilled. Most items are best enjoyed within 24–48 hours and should be kept refrigerated until then.

Do you list allergens?

Yes, every product page lists full ingredients alongside calories, sugar, and protein, so you can check for common allergens like dairy, nuts, or gluten before ordering.

Can I order for a large event, like a wedding or office party?

Yes, see our Event Specials above. Submit a quotation request with your event date, headcount, and menu preference, and our team will get back to you with pricing.

Is there a subscription or repeat-order option?

Not yet, but it's on our roadmap. For now, our bundles and packages are the best way to stock up and save on repeat orders.
